# Exploratory Analysis Findings

## pH

Average pH remained relatively stable across the monitored River Thames sections between 2021 and 2025. Recorded annual averages were generally close to pH 8, indicating limited variation across years and locations.

The main observations were:

- Reading to Cookham recorded the highest average shown, at 8.18 in 2021.
- Egham to Teddington recorded the lowest average, at 7.96 in 2023.
- The 2023 results contained several of the lower annual averages.
- Differences between the four river sections were generally small.
- No consistent upward or downward pattern was observed across the full period.

### Portfolio Insight

pH conditions remained stable across the monitored River Thames sections from 2021 to 2025. Although a small reduction appeared in some sections during 2023, the results do not indicate a sustained change across the study period.

### Interpretation Caution

These results summarise annual averages from the available monitoring observations. They may conceal short-term fluctuations and should be interpreted alongside differences in sampling frequency and monthly coverage.

The pH analysis is exploratory and should not be treated as a formal regulatory water-quality classification. 

## Dissolved Oxygen, Percentage Saturation

Average dissolved oxygen saturation remained high across the monitored river sections between 2021 and 2025, with annual averages generally ranging from approximately 92% to 103%.

The main observations were:

 Cookham to Egham remained close to 100% saturation across the period.
 Egham to Teddington recorded the highest annual average, at 102.92% in 2022.
 Egham to Teddington declined to 91.65% in 2023 before recovering to 99.03% in 2025.
 Wallingford to Caversham increased from 97.28% in 2023 to 101.31% in 2025.
 The results show annual variation, but no consistent portfolio-wide decline.

### Portfolio Insight

> Dissolved oxygen saturation remained relatively high across the monitored river sections. A temporary reduction occurred at Egham to Teddington in 2023, followed by recovery, while Wallingford to Caversham showed an increase between 2023 and 2025.

### Interpretation Caution

The figures represent annual averages calculated from available observations. Annual averages may conceal short-term low-oxygen events, seasonal variation and differences in sampling frequency.

The results should therefore not be interpreted as evidence that every individual observation or monitoring period had equally favourable oxygen conditions.


## Orthophosphate, Reactive as Phosphorus

Average orthophosphate concentrations generally declined across most monitored river sections between 2021 and 2025, although the pattern was not consistent everywhere.

The main observations were:

- Cookham to Egham decreased from 0.23 mg/L in 2021 to 0.14 mg/L in 2025.
- Egham to Teddington decreased from 0.21 mg/L in 2021 to 0.13 mg/L in 2024, before increasing to 0.16 mg/L in 2025.
- Reading to Cookham remained comparatively stable, ranging from approximately 0.11 to 0.16 mg/L.
- Wallingford to Caversham decreased to 0.14 mg/L in 2024 but increased to 0.18 mg/L in 2025.
- The 2025 increases at Egham to Teddington and Wallingford to Caversham warrant continued monitoring.

### Portfolio Insight

Orthophosphate concentrations show an overall improvement across much of the study area, particularly at Cookham to Egham. However, increases recorded in some river sections during 2025 indicate that nutrient pressures remain variable and should continue to be monitored.

### Interpretation Caution

Annual averages may conceal seasonal peaks, individual elevated measurements and differences in sampling frequency.

The results indicate patterns in the available monitoring data and do not establish the sources or causes of phosphorus concentrations.
